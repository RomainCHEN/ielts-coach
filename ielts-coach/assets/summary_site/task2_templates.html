🏗️ 万能四段结构（所有题型通用）

1
Introduction 开头
40-50 词
改写题目 + 亮出明确立场（审题最关键）
2
Body 1 主体段1
80-100 词
主观点 1：观点句 + 解释 + 具体例子
3
Body 2 主体段2
80-100 词
主观点 2（或让步反驳）+ 解释 + 例子
4
Conclusion 结尾
30-40 词
换词重申立场 + 总结论证逻辑（不引新观点）
⚠️ 写给 7.0 的你：定位是关键
  • ✅ 立场要贯穿全文：开头定了立场，主体段和结尾要持续强化，不能模糊。
  • ✅ 每个主体段一个中心观点：观点句开头，解释展开，例子支撑，不跑题。
  • ✅ 例子必须具体：真实案例 / 你自己的观察 / 你练过的题材，不要空泛。
  • ❌ 开头 40 词以内必须出现你的立场，别拖到结尾才说。
  • ❌ 结尾绝不引入新观点，只总结。

🗣️ Opinion 观点题（To what extent do you agree or disagree?）

🗣️
高频指数 ★★★★★ · 考得最多
看到 "To what extent do you agree or disagree" / "Do you agree that..." / "Is this a positive or negative development"
🔍 一眼认出它
To what extent do you agree or disagree? · Do you agree that...? · Do the advantages outweigh...?(见利弊题) · 题目有明确的"观点"等你表态
✏️ 开头（40-50词 · 必须表态）

The question of whether X 应该做某事 has attracted considerable debate. While I accept that 承认反方一个优点, I would argue that 你的立场.

变体 1（彻底同意）：I agree with this view wholeheartedly, for two main reasons.

变体 2（8分式微调）：I largely agree, although I would add one important caveat about 限定条件.

🛡️ 主体段1（主理由）

The main reason I take this view is that 理由1. 一句话解释为什么. For example, 具体例子. This suggests that 回扣立场.

⚔️ 主体段2（让步 + 反驳）

Nevertheless, it would be wrong to ignore 反方观点. Those who 支持反方的人 point to 反方理由. However, in my experience, 反驳（用例子/观察）.

🎯 结尾（30-40词 · 换词重申）

In conclusion, while there is some merit in the opposing view, I remain convinced that 用不同词重申立场, because 一句话总结核心理由.

变体：Ultimately, 立场. Whatever one's view, the evidence points in this direction.

🎯 观点题专属表达 （表态用词）

I would argue that / I am convinced that我认为（直接明确）
I largely agree, with one caveat大体同意，有一个保留
the question of whether ... has attracted debate是否...引发了争论
while it is true that ..., I believe ...虽然...属实，但我认为...
I take the view that / my position is that我的立场是
I am firmly of the opinion that我坚定地认为
the main reason I take this view is that我持此观点的主要原因是
there is some merit in ......确实有可取之处
the evidence points in this direction证据指向这个方向
wholeheartedly / firmly / strongly完全地 / 坚定地（程度副词）
💡 你练过的类似题：AI 将彻底改变教育（AI's impact on education）

Intro: The question of whether artificial intelligence will fundamentally transform education has attracted considerable debate. While I accept that traditional teachers will always have a role, I would argue that AI will reshape how students learn in profound ways.

Body1 开头句: The main reason I take this view is that AI makes personalised learning genuinely possible. A system can now analyse a student's weak points and adapt exercises in real time, something no human teacher could do for forty students at once.

📌 记住：观点题第一段就亮明立场（"I would argue"），比 "In my opinion" 更书面；让步段用"while it is true that"再反驳，结构就完整了。

💬 Discussion 讨论题（Discuss both views and give your opinion）

💬
高频指数 ★★★★☆ · 两个观点都要谈
看到 "Discuss both views and give your opinion" / "Some people think X, others believe Y"
🔍 一眼认出它
Discuss both views and give your own opinion. 题目明确给了两种对立观点，两方都必须讨论，最后表态。
✏️ 开头（40-50词 · 提两方 + 表态）

There is an ongoing debate about whether 观点A or 观点B is preferable. Supporters of the former emphasise A的理由, whereas proponents of the latter point to B的理由. In my view, 你的立场.

🅰️ 主体段1（讨论一方）

Those who advocate 观点A argue that A的理由. A case in point is 例子. This view has considerable appeal, since 为什么有道理.

🅱️ 主体段2（讨论另一方）

By contrast, proponents of 观点B contend that B的理由. For instance, 例子. This is particularly true in 具体情境.

🎯 结尾（30-40词 · 明确表态）

Ultimately, although both perspectives have merit, I believe that 你的立场, because 一句核心理由. This is not to dismiss the other view entirely, but the balance of evidence favours it.

🎯 讨论题专属表达 （两方切换不机械）

those who advocate X argue that ...支持X的人认为
proponents of ... / opponents contend that ...支持者 / 反对者主张
supporters of the former ... / the latter前者 / 后者（替代重复）
this view has considerable appeal这个观点很有说服力
I find the opposing view more persuasive我觉得反方更有说服力
both perspectives have merit两方都有道理
the balance of evidence favours ...证据的天平倾向...
a case in point is ...一个典型例子是
this is not to dismiss ... entirely这并不是完全否定...
underpins / reinforces their position支撑 / 强化他们的立场
💡 你练过的类似题：社交媒体对年轻人心理健康（Social media & youth mental health）

Intro: There is an ongoing debate about whether social media harms young people's mental health or brings more good than harm. Supporters of the former emphasise constant comparison and anxiety, whereas proponents of the latter point to connection and community. In my view, the negative effects are more significant.

📌 讨论题最容易犯的错：只谈一方。两方各一段，每段都配例子，最后一句明确"我站哪边"。别用 "On the one hand / On the other hand" 这种机械切换（见反AI味自查）。

🛠️ Problem-Solution 问题解决题（What problems does X cause? What solutions...?）

🛠️
高频指数 ★★★★☆ · 问题 + 对策各占一段
看到 "What problems does X cause?" / "Why is this happening? What can be done?"
🔍 一眼认出它
What problems does ... cause? · What solutions can you suggest? · What measures should be taken? 题干直接问"问题"和"对策"。
✏️ 开头（40-50词 · 点出严重性）

X 现象 has become an increasingly serious concern in recent years. 一句话说明为什么值得关注. A range of measures are therefore needed to tackle this issue.

🩹 主体段1（问题段）

One of the most serious problems associated with X is 问题1. 解释问题的严重性. For example, 数据/案例. 如果有必要再加问题2.

💊 主体段2（对策段）

The most effective way to address this would be for 主体（政府/学校/个人） to 对策. 对策怎么起作用. If this were implemented, 预期效果.

🎯 结尾（30-40词）

To sum up, although X presents considerable challenges, they are not insurmountable. With a combination of 对策1 and 对策2, the situation could be significantly improved.

🎯 问题解决题专属表达

has become an increasingly serious concern已成为日益严重的问题
the most effective way to address this is ...解决这个最有效的办法是
presents considerable challenges带来相当大的挑战
not insurmountable并非不可逾越
measures should be taken / implemented应该采取措施
if this were implemented, ...如果这项措施得以实施（虚拟语气加分）
one of the most serious problems is ...最严重的问题之一是
governments could legislate / fund / subsidise政府可以立法 / 资助 / 补贴
raise public awareness through campaigns通过活动提高公众意识
tackle / address / remedy the problem解决 / 处理 / 补救问题
💡 你练过的类似题：动物灭绝（Animal Extinction）

Problem 段开头: One of the most serious problems associated with biodiversity loss is the accelerating rate of habitat destruction, driven largely by deforestation and urban expansion. As natural habitats shrink, countless species face extinction within decades.

Solution 段开头: The most effective way to address this would be for governments to designate more protected areas and strictly enforce anti-poaching laws. If this were implemented alongside public education, endangered populations could begin to recover.

📌 结构铁律：问题段别把每个问题都展开，重点写透 1-2 个；对策段必须有"谁来管（政府/学校/个人）+ 怎么做 + 预期效果"，一个对策写透比列三个空洞对策好。

⚖️ Advantages / Disadvantages 利弊题（Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?）

⚖️
高频指数 ★★★☆☆ · 必须表态"哪边更重"
看到 "Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?" / "What are the advantages and disadvantages of X?"
🔍 一眼认出它
Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? · What are the advantages and disadvantages of ...? 关键词 advantages / disadvantages / benefits / drawbacks。
✏️ 开头（40-50词 · 亮明哪边重）

Like most developments, X brings both benefits and drawbacks. While 简单承认反方, I would argue that the advantages / disadvantages are more significant.

变体（利大于弊）：On balance, the benefits of X outweigh its drawbacks, for reasons explained below.

📈 主体段1（利或弊）

On the benefit side, 好处1. 解释. For instance, 例子. This enables 谁 to 结果.

📉 主体段2（另一面）

Against this, there are also real drawbacks. 坏处. 解释严重性. For example, 例子.

🎯 结尾（30-40词 · 明确"重"在哪边）

In conclusion, even though X has certain advantages / disadvantages, I am convinced that the 另一边 are more considerable, because 一句话核心理由.

🎯 利弊题专属表达 （权衡语气）

the benefits outweigh the drawbacks利大于弊
on balance / on the whole总的来说
a double-edged sword双刃剑
brings both benefits and drawbacks带来利与弊
one notable advantage / drawback is ...一个显著的好处 / 坏处是
the advantages are more considerable优势更为显著
against this, there are real drawbacks与此相对，确实存在坏处
I am convinced that the balance tips toward ...我确信天平倾向...
long-term gains vs short-term costs长期收益 vs 短期代价
mitigate the drawbacks减轻弊端
💡 你练过的类似题：出国留学（Advantages and disadvantages of studying abroad）

Intro: Like most major life choices, studying abroad brings both benefits and drawbacks. While it can be expensive and lonely, I would argue that the advantages are more significant for a young person's growth.

结尾: In conclusion, even though studying overseas has certain drawbacks, I am convinced that the benefits are more considerable, because the independence and global perspective it cultivates are hard to gain any other way.

📌 关键：题问"哪边 outweigh"，结尾必须明确回答"哪边更重"，不能只说"两边都有"。你出国读研的亲身计划就是最好的例子素材。

❓ Two-part 双问题题（Why...? What can be done...?）

❓
高频指数 ★★★☆☆ · 一个题目问两个问题
题干里有两个问号："Why is this?" + "What can be done?" 一个主体段回答一个问题
🔍 一眼认出它
题目同时问两个问题（一个问原因，一个问对策）："Why is this the case? What can be done about it?" 两个问题都要各占一段回答。
✏️ 开头（40-50词 · 点题+预告两部分）

X 现象 has become increasingly common. Understanding why this has happened, and what can be done about it, is therefore important.

❓ 主体段1（回答第一个问题 · 原因）

The reasons for this are varied, but one of the most significant is 原因. 解释. This is borne out by 例子/观察. A further contributing factor is 次要原因.

💡 主体段2（回答第二个问题 · 对策）

Turning to possible responses, 对策 would help, because 为什么有效. For example, 案例. More broadly, 更大范围的对策.

🎯 结尾（30-40词 · 两个问题各一句）

In summary, X 现象 can be attributed mainly to 原因, and the most promising way forward is 对策. With sustained effort, the situation can be improved.

🎯 双问题专属表达 （原因 + 对策切换）

the reasons for this are varied原因多种多样
one of the most significant is ...其中一个最主要的是
a further contributing factor is ...另一个促成因素是
this is borne out by ...这一点由...证实
turning to possible responses, ...说到可能的对策
the most promising way forward is ...最有希望的前进方向是
can be attributed mainly to ...主要归因于
with sustained effort通过持续努力
stem from / arise from / result from源于 / 由...引起（原因动词）
would go a long way toward ...对...大有帮助
💡 你练过的类似题：年轻人更多待在室内（Outdoor Activities / 儿童户外活动减少）

原因段开头: The reasons for this are varied, but one of the most significant is the rise of digital entertainment. A child today can be endlessly absorbed by a tablet, which is far more immediately rewarding than going outside to play.

对策段开头: Turning to possible responses, redesigning school curriculums to guarantee outdoor time would go a long way toward reversing the trend, because regular, structured outdoor play would become a habit rather than an exception.

📌 双问题最容易翻车：只回答了一个问题，或两个问题混在一起写。秘诀：开头明确写"我讲两件事：原因 + 对策"，然后主体段各答一问，结尾各总结一句。

🚫 反 AI 味自查（考官能认出 AI 作文，必须避开）

AI 味模式（避开）自然人类写法（用这个）
Em dash（—）用逗号、冒号、分号或拆句。你的整篇作文里 em dash 应为 0 个。
"In today's modern society..." / "In this day and age..."直接说事："Childhood obesity has risen sharply since the early 2000s."
"It is noteworthy that..." / "It is worth mentioning that..."直接陈述事实，不要"值得注意"的废话开场。
"This essay will discuss..." / "This essay will examine..."直接给立场："I believe that... because..."
"Firstly, Secondly, Finally" 流水账"The first factor to consider is..." / "Equally important is..." / "Beyond these concerns..."
"On the one hand... on the other hand" 反复用"While some argue X, others contend Y" / "The case for X is strong, yet Y presents a different picture."
"Not only... but also..." 用两次以上换强调结构："What matters most is..." / "Equally crucial is..."
"In conclusion" 后逐字重复开头换词总结："Ultimately..." / "Taken together..." / 或直接自然收束。
滥用引号 "so-called" 讽刺语正常直接说这个词，不要加引号。
空泛抽象名词堆砌（"the phenomenon of globalization"）用具体例子接地气，从你的真实观察出发。
💡 为什么这条对你 7.0 至关重要
  • Lexical Resource：机械连词（Firstly/Secondly）是 Band 6 的信号，Band 7 要求衔接"灵活、自然"。
  • 真实感：AI 味越重，考官越觉得你在背模板，Coherence 印象分会受影响。
  • 好习惯：长短句交错（一句短一句长）、具体例子、直接观点，这些让文章像"人在写"。

🔗 Band 6 → 7 的分水岭：衔接方式

🚫 Band 6（机械衔接）

"Firstly, ... Secondly, ... Finally, ..."

"On the one hand... On the other hand..."

每句都用连接词开头，像套了链子。

✅ Band 7（灵活衔接）

用引用（reference）：this, these, such, the former, the latter

用替代（substitution）：one, do so

句子之间靠逻辑自然流动，不是靠胶水连词。

📌 引用 + 替代 = Band 7 的秘密武器 （你已经会用，现在有意识地用）

This / These指代上文观点："Many cities restrict cars. This has reduced congestion."
Such + 名词"Students who study online save time, but such learners may lack discipline."
the former / the latter指代前一句提到的两者，避免重复。
one / do so替代："Some prefer traditional classrooms; others do not."
this, in turn, means that ...层层递进，逻辑自然衔接。
which is why ..."Exams create stress, which is why some favour continuous assessment."

🎯 少量高质量连词就够 （每段 1-2 个即可，别堆）

Furthermore / Moreover此外（递进）
By contrast / In contrast相比之下（对比）
Admittedly / Granted诚然（让步）
Nevertheless / Yet然而（转折）
As a result / Consequently因此（结果）
In practice / In reality实际上（收束到现实）

💡 一组万能"开头句式"清单（避免每题都背同一句）

1. Whether ... is a question that divides opinion.

2. The way we approach ... says a lot about our priorities.

3. Few would dispute that ...; the disagreement lies in how to respond.

4. It is tempting to assume ..., but the reality is more complicated.

📌 考前把这些句式混着用，避免每篇开头都一样（雷同本身就是 AI 味）。
