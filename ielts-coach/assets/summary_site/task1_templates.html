🏗️ 万能四段结构（所有题型通用）

1
Introduction 开头
1 句：改写题目，不照抄。Time + Chart Type + What
2
Overview 概述
2-3 句：最大趋势 / 对比结论。⚠️ 不写具体数据
3
Detail 1 细节段1
3-4 句：第一组特征 + 数据支撑
4
Detail 2 细节段2
3-4 句：剩余特征 + 对比
⚠️ Task 1 铁律：只描述数据，绝不解释原因
  • ✅ 安全："The figure increased from 5% to 12% between 2000 and 2010."
  • ✅ 安全："While A fell by half, B rose steadily over the same period."
  • ❌ 危险：解释原因（"because of..."）
  • ❌ 危险：预测未来（"this trend will continue..."）
  • ❌ 危险：评价好坏（"this is a worrying trend"）
  • ❌ 危险：概述段里写具体数字

📈 Line Graph 折线图

📈
趋势描述题
重点：上升 / 下降 / 波动 / 峰值 / 平稳 · 一般有时间轴（years）
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The line graph illustrates how the number of X changed over a period of XX years, from year to year.

Alternative: The graph depicts the trend in X between year and year.

🔍 概述 Overview（无数据）

Overall, X followed a broadly upward/declining trajectory, while Y remained relatively stable throughout the period.

Another option: Overall, the most striking feature is the sharp rise in X, which contrasted with the gradual fall in Y.

🔢 细节段开头句

In year, X stood at value, before climbing steadily to a peak of value in year.

By contrast, Y started at value and then plummeted to a low of value.

🔄 趋势衔接句

For the remainder of the period, the figure fluctuated between low and high, before levelling off at value.

After an initial dip, the numbers recovered and went on to surpass their earlier peak.

🎯 折线图专属词汇 （按强度排序）

rose / increased / climbed上升
soared / surged猛涨
plummeted / plunged暴跌
dipped / dipped slightly小幅下跌
fluctuated / oscillated波动
peaked at / reached a high of达到峰值
bottomed out at / hit a low of触底
levelled off / plateaued趋于平稳
remained stable / stayed constant保持不变
an upward/downward trend上升/下降趋势
steadily / gradually / sharply稳步 / 逐渐 / 急剧（副词）
over the same period在同一时期
💡 完整示例（UK Transport 2000-2020 已练过）

The line graph shows how the number of passengers using four transport modes in the UK changed between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, rail travel saw the most dramatic increase, while local bus journeys declined steadily over the two decades.

In 2000, around 2 million trips were made by rail, a figure that rose sharply to roughly 5 million by 2020. Cycling also gained popularity, tripling from 0.5 to 1.5 million over the same period. Bus travel, in contrast, followed the opposite path, falling from 3 million to below 2 million.

📌 记住节奏：概述给"对比 + 总趋势"，细节段按"一组上升 + 一组下降"分组展开。

📊 Bar Chart 柱状图

📊
对比描述题
重点：谁最高 / 谁最低 / 相差多少 · 有时间变化时也写趋势
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The bar chart compares the proportion/number of X across categories in year(s).

Alternative: The chart provides a breakdown of X by category, measured in units.

🔍 概述 Overview

Overall, Category A accounted for the largest share, whereas Category B recorded the lowest figure in every year shown.

Another option: Overall, there was a clear gap between A and B, and this difference widened over the period.

🔢 细节段开头句

A was by far the highest, at value, followed by B with value.

A outnumbered B by margin, while C was the least popular, standing at just value.

🔄 对比衔接句

In contrast, B experienced an opposite trend, rising from value to value.

Similarly / Likewise, C remained consistently low at around value.

Compared with year, the figure for D had roughly doubled by year.

🎯 柱状图专属词汇 （对比/占比优先）

accounted for / comprised占比
the highest / the lowest figure最高 / 最低值
significantly more/less than明显多于/少于
by far the most/least遥遥领先/垫底
outnumbered / was exceeded by超过 / 被超过
roughly / approximately大约（数据模糊化）
nearly / just under接近 / 略低于
three times as high as是...的三倍
double / triple翻倍 / 翻三倍
in contrast / whereas / compared to对比连接
a clear gap between A and BA和B差距明显
followed by其次是（排序用）
💡 完整示例（European Income Spending 已练过）

The bar chart compares household spending across several European countries between 1998 and 2008.

Overall, spending rose in nearly every country, with France recording the highest figures in both years and Poland the lowest.

France led the way at around 1,900 euros in 1998, far ahead of its nearest rival. By 2008 its figure had climbed to roughly 2,300 euros. Poland, by contrast, spent the least of all, although it also saw the steepest proportional rise, from about 400 to nearly 800 euros.

📌 柱状图 = 先说谁最高谁最低（概述），再挑 2-3 个有对比意义的数字展开。

🥧 Pie Chart 饼图

🥧
占比描述题
重点：百分比词汇 · 最大/最小份额 · 多个饼图对比（常考2-3个年份）
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The pie charts show how X was divided among categories in year 1 and year 2.

Alternative: The charts illustrate the proportion of X attributable to each category over two time periods.

🔍 概述 Overview

Overall, A made up the bulk of X in both years, whereas B accounted for only a small fraction.

Another option: Overall, the most notable change was the substantial growth in A's share, which came largely at the expense of B.

🔢 细节段开头句

In year, A was the dominant category, accounting for XX%, more than double that of B, which stood at XX%.

The remaining categories were split fairly evenly, each hovering at around XX%.

🔄 对比衔接句

By year, the balance had shifted: A's share fell to XX%, while B grew to XX%.

Most categories changed little, but C halved from XX% to XX%.

🎯 饼图专属词汇 （百分比描述）

accounted for / represented占
the majority / a minority of大多数 / 少数
the bulk of / the lion's share绝大部分
a small fraction / a tiny proportion很小一部分
approximately / roughly / around大约
just over / just under / almost略高于 / 略低于 / 几乎
more than double / half of超过两倍 / 一半
at the expense of以...为代价（份额转移）
the balance had shifted格局发生转变
shared equally / split evenly平分 / 均匀分配
came close behind紧随其后
remained the same / unchanged保持不变
💡 完整示例（Sydney Water Use 已练过）

The pie charts compare how water was used in different sectors in Sydney in 1997 and 2007.

Overall, households were the largest consumers of water in both years, while industry used the least.

Domestic use accounted for roughly 35% of the total in 1997 and had risen to about 45% by 2007, an increase of ten percentage points. Industry, by contrast, fell from around 25% to just over 10% over the same decade, a decline that more than halved its share.

📌 饼图铁律：每个数据都必须是百分比 + 一定要用模糊表达（approximately/around），别照抄精确数字。

📋 Table 表格

📋
多维数据题
重点：不能逐行念数字 · 必须挑最值 + 排序 + 对比 · 常考国家/年份对比
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The table presents data on X in places, broken down by category for year(s).

Alternative: The table gives an overview of X across places, with figures measured in units.

🔍 概述 Overview

Overall, A recorded the highest figures across all categories, while B consistently trailed at the bottom.

Another option: Overall, the table reveals wide disparities between places, with the difference being most pronounced in category.

🔢 细节段开头句（排序法）

At the top of the table, A reported value, well ahead of B at value. C came third, with value.

By contrast, D had the lowest figure of value, only about a fifth of A's total.

🔄 细节段开头句（对比法）

In terms of category 1, A led with value, followed by B at value.

As far as category 2 is concerned, however, the order was reversed, with B exceeding A by margin.

🎯 表格专属词汇 （排序 & 比较）

at the top / bottom of the table位于榜首 / 垫底
recorded the highest/lowest figure记录最高/最低值
well ahead of / trailed behind远超 / 落后于
in terms of / as far as ... is concerned就...而言
the order was reversed次序颠倒
a fifth / a quarter / half of几分之几表达
wide disparities between巨大差异
remained unchanged / stayed constant维持不变
exceeded / was exceeded by超过 / 被超越
slightly higher/lower than略高/低于
ranged from ... to ...范围从...到...
on average / typically平均 / 通常
💡 完整示例（Medical Care 三国对比 已练过）

The table provides data on medical care across three European countries over a ten-year period.

Overall, Country A consistently outperformed the other two in most indicators, while Country C had the least favourable figures throughout.

At the top of the table, Country A reported 85 doctors per 100,000 people, well ahead of Country B's 70 and Country C's 52. The gap was even wider in hospital beds, where Country A had 6.2 per 1,000 residents, roughly double Country C's figure.

📌 表格最容易写成"清单"，务必用 排序（top/middle/bottom）或 维度对比（in terms of X）来分组，永远别逐行读。

⚙️ Process Diagram 流程图

⚙️
流程描述题
重点：阶段词 first/subsequently/finally · 被动语态 · 概括段写"总共几个阶段+起点终点"
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The diagram outlines the process by which X is produced / manufactured, from raw material to final product.

Alternative: The illustration shows the various stages involved in X, starting with A and ending with B.

🔍 概述 Overview（无细节）

Overall, the process consists of X distinct stages, beginning with A and concluding with B.

Another option: Overall, it is a largely linear/manual/automated process with X steps, although stage involves some recycling/reuse.

For natural cycles: Overall, the cycle repeats itself, passing through X main phases.

🔢 细节段开头句（第一段）

In the first stage, raw material is collected and then transported to a processing facility.

At the start of the process, the material is heated in a container, during which time it turns into substance.

Once X has been completed, the output moves to the next stage.

🔄 细节段开头句（衔接）

Subsequently / Following this, the material is combined with Y and pressed into shape.

At the next stage, the mixture is poured into moulds, where it sets and is then removed for packaging.

In the final stage, the finished product is packaged and distributed to retailers.

🎯 流程图专属词汇 （阶段词 + 动词）

initially / at the start of the process首先
subsequently / following this接着
at the next stage / at this point下一步 / 此时
once ... has been done一旦...完成后
in the final stage / finally最后
is collected / transported / processed被收集 / 运输 / 加工（被动）
is heated / pressed / poured / moulded被加热 / 压制 / 灌注 / 塑形
turns into / is converted into转变为
is combined with / mixed with与...混合
is packaged and distributed包装并分销
the cycle repeats itself循环往复（自然循环）
raw material / by-product / end product原料 / 副产品 / 成品
💡 完整示例（Carbonated Drinks Process / Stone Age Tools 已练过）

The diagram outlines the process by which soft drinks are produced, from the sourcing of water to the finished bottles on shelves.

Overall, the process involves five main stages, beginning with water treatment and ending with the distribution of the packaged product.

At the start of the process, raw water is filtered and treated with chemicals to remove impurities. It is then combined with flavouring and carbon dioxide in a large mixing tank. Following this, the mixture is bottled, sealed, and finally distributed to shops for sale.

📌 流程图三大要点：全程被动语态、不写时间/数量、概述必须写"共几个阶段+起终点"。已经练过碳饮料和石器工具两个流程题，这个很熟了。

🗺️ Map 地图

🗺️
变化对比题
重点：was replaced by / converted into · 方位词 north/south · 前后对比（now vs before）
✏️ 开头改写 Introduction

The maps illustrate the development of a place between year 1 and year 2, showing how the area changed over time.

Alternative: The two diagrams compare the layout of place in year 1 with its present-day arrangement.

🔍 概述 Overview

Overall, the area underwent significant redevelopment, with most of the old features giving way to modern facilities.

Another option: Overall, the most striking change was the expansion of X, which now occupies an area that was formerly use.

If little changed: Overall, while X remained virtually unchanged, Y was completely transformed.

🔢 细节段开头句（变化）

In the north-eastern corner of the map, the old factory was demolished and replaced by a new housing estate.

The carpark to the south of the main road was converted into a shopping centre.

The river bank, which had been undeveloped, now features a park.

🔄 细节段开头句（保持不变）

By contrast, the station in the centre of the map remained in its original location, though it was extended.

The existing X was retained, whereas the Y that had previously occupied the northern area was removed.

🎯 地图专属词汇 （方位 + 变化）

was replaced by / gave way to被替代 / 让位于
was converted into / transformed into被改造成
was demolished / removed / knocked down被拆除
was constructed / built / added被新建
remained unchanged / was retained保持不变 / 保留
underwent significant redevelopment经历大规模改造
expanded / extended扩建
in the north-east / south-west corner东北角 / 西南角
to the north/south/east/west of在...的北/南/东/西
adjacent to / next to / near紧邻 / 附近
surrounded by被...环绕
virtually unchanged几乎没变
💡 完整示例（Museum Floor Plans 已练过）

The two maps show how the layout of a museum changed between 1998 and 2008.

Overall, the museum underwent considerable redevelopment, with the entrance moving and several new exhibition areas being created.

To the north of the building, the old entrance hall was relocated to the east side, and the former ticket office was converted into a gift shop. In the centre, what had been a temporary exhibition space gave way to a permanent gallery, while the café, which had been confined to the south-west corner, was extended along the southern wall.

📌 地图：概述写"大变化方向"，细节段按"哪里变了 / 哪里没变"分组，方位词必须准确。

🚫 常见扣分点自查清单

✗
概述段带具体数字 — 概述只写趋势和结论，数据留给细节段。
✓ 改正：Overview 里写 "rose sharply"，不写 "from 5% to 15%"。
✗
照抄题目原句 — 开头必须改写（paraphrase），直接抄会扣 Lexical Resource 分。
✓ 改正：换个动词（illustrates→shows）+ 换个句式。
✗
解释原因 / 预测未来 — Task 1 只描述，不分析。
✓ 改正：删掉 "because..." "this suggests..." "will continue..."。
✗
逐行 / 逐项念数据 — 表格和饼图最常犯，没有信息分组。
✓ 改正：先排高低，再挑最值得写的 2-3 组对比。
✗
数据精确到小数 — 口语化夸张反而更真实，考官看描述能力不看复读。
✓ 改正：用 approximately / just over / roughly。
✗
地图/流程图用了主动语态 — 流程和变化描述应大量用被动语态。
✓ 改正："the building was demolished" 而不是 "they demolished the building"。
✗
一个动词反复用 — increase 从头用到尾。
✓ 改正：rise / climb / grow / surge / soar 轮换。
✗
字数不足 150 — 写到 160-180 词最安全。
✓ 改正：细节段两组对比展开即可达标。
✗
用 em dash（—） — 强 AI 信号，考官容易识别。
✓ 改正：改用逗号、冒号、句号。
