⏱️ 答题节奏速记

Part 1 · 日常问答
每题 20-40 秒 · 2-4 句话
别背长答案！直接回答 → 加一个例子 → 一句感受，就够。答完就停，别硬凑。
Part 2 · 1分钟准备 + 2分钟说
约 250 词 · 全程说满
准备时只写 4-5 个关键词（谁/哪/什么/为什么）。开场直接点题，每个 cue point 都覆盖，结尾一句收束。
Part 3 · 深度讨论
每题 60-80 秒 · 3-5 句话
观点 → 理由 → 例子，三件套。用正式一点的词汇，可以加"但是/另一方面"体现思辨。

1️⃣ Part 1 逻辑链条（4 类话题）

A. 偏好 / 习惯题（Music, Food, Shopping, Hobby, Reading, Cars...）
最常考，占 Part 1 一半以上
直接表态 → 一个具体例子 → 原因/感受
I'd say I'm really into...我很喜欢开场直接回答
I gravitate towards...我倾向于比 like 高级
To be honest, ...说实话拉近距离
That's partly because...部分原因是补原因
It's my go-to when I need to unwind.这是我放松时的首选万能收尾
I'm a huge fan of...我是...的超级粉丝
I've always been drawn to...我一直被...吸引
It's grown on me over the years.这些年我越来越喜欢它谈变化
I used to ... but now I'm more into ...我以前...但现在更...谈转变
B. 描述题（Hometown, Home, The Area/City You Live In, Building, Scenery...）
让你描述一个地方或事物
总体印象 → 一个具体细节 → 你的感受
It's quite... / It has a really ... feel to it它相当.../很有...的感觉
What I love most is...我最喜欢的是
The best part is...最好的部分是
You'd never guess, but...你绝对想不到引出细节
It's one of those places that ...它是那种...的地方
There's a real sense of ... about it.它有一种...的感觉
You can feel the ... as soon as you arrive.一到就能感受到
It's surrounded by ... / It's famous for ...它被...环绕 / 以...闻名
C. 回忆题（Childhood, Memory, Pets, Gifts, Life stages...）
让你回忆过去
一个具体回忆 → 细节画面 → 现在的感受
When I was younger, ...我小时候
I still remember the day when...我仍记得那一天
Looking back, ...回头看
It's one of my earliest memories.这是我最早期的记忆之一
I have vivid memories of ...我对...有清晰的记忆
It brings back memories of ...它让我想起...
Those were simpler times.那是更单纯的时光
D. 观点题（Social media, Science, Websites, Parks...）
让你表达看法
立场 → 一个理由 → 一个例子
I genuinely think...我真的认为
From my point of view, ...在我看来
What I've noticed is...我注意到的是
That said, I can see the other side too.不过我也理解另一面体现思辨
If you ask me, ...要我说
There's no doubt that ...毫无疑问
It's a double-edged sword.这是双刃剑

2️⃣ Part 2 逻辑链条（5 类话题）

A. 人物题（A Person Who..., A Friend, A Teacher, A Famous Person...）
12 个你练过的人物话题，全是这个套路
WHO + 关系 → 一个具体故事/特质 → 对你的影响/你的感受
The person who comes to mind is...我想到的人是
What makes him/her special is...他/她特别之处在于
There was this one time when...有这么一次引出故事
To this day, he/she remains...至今他/她仍是
I first met him/her when ...我第一次见到他/她是...
He/She has this quality of ...他/她有一种...的特质
I've always looked up to ...我一直仰慕...
B. 地点题（A Place to Recommend, A Home to Visit, A City You Visited...）
地点题的灵魂是"画面感"
WHERE + 描述 → 你在那做了什么/经历 → 为什么喜欢/重要
It's located in... / not far from...它位于
The moment you step in, ...一踏进去就...
What I remember most is...我最记得的是
It holds a special place in my heart.它在我心中有特殊位置
It's the kind of place where you can ...这是那种你可以...的地方
The atmosphere there is ...那里的氛围是...
It's off the beaten track.它很冷门/人迹罕至高分表达
C. 物品题（A Piece of Technology, A Special Cake, An Item Cost More...）
物品题的灵魂是"它对我的意义"
WHAT + 描述 → 怎么得到/为什么买 → 意义/价值
It's basically a ... that ...它基本上是一个...
I got it / bought it when...我是在...时候得到/买的
What makes it special is...它特别在
I can't imagine my life without it.无法想象没有它的生活
It's not the most expensive, but ...它不是最贵的，但...
It's been with me through ...它陪伴我经历了...
Every time I see it, it reminds me of ...每次看到它，就让我想起
D. 经历题（A Time When..., An Occasion..., A Day Out...）
15 个你练过的经历题，用时间线讲故事
WHEN/WHERE 开场 → 过程（起→中→末） → 感悟/收获
This happened ... last year/a few weeks ago发生在...
At first, ... Then, ... Finally, ...起初...然后...最后
What surprised me most was...最让我惊讶的是
Looking back, it taught me that...回头看，它教会我
It all started when ...一切始于...
Little did I know that ...我当时完全没想到制造悬念
It turned out to be ...结果竟然是
E. 抽象/计划题（A Law, An Important Decision, A Long-term Goal, An Opinion Change...）
抽象题的灵魂是"具体化到一个例子"
定义/点题 → 具体化（一个例子） → 为什么/意义
One [decision/change] that stands out is...一个突出的...是
Let me give you a concrete example.让我举个具体例子
This matters because...这很重要因为
In the long run, ...从长远看
It's hard to put into words, but ...很难用语言表达，但万能开场
The key turning point was ...关键的转折点是
It all comes down to ...归根到底是

3️⃣ Part 3 逻辑链条（4 类问题）

A. 观点分析题（Why...? / Do you think...?）
观点 → 理由 → 例子 → 反向思考（可选）
I genuinely believe that...我真的相信
The main reason is that...主要原因是
A case in point is...一个例子是
Having said that, ...话虽如此引出另一面
I'm in two minds about this.我对此有些矛盾
The way I see it, ...在我看来
It boils down to ...归结起来是
B. 对比题（What's the difference between...?）
一方特点 → 另一方特点 → 总结差异
The contrast is quite striking.对比相当鲜明
On the one hand... / On the other hand...一方面/另一方面
whereas / while / by contrast而/相比之下
In general, I'd say...总的来说
There's a world of difference between ......之间有天壤之别
X is in a league of its own.X 独树一帜
The gap is narrowing / widening.差距在缩小/扩大
C. 趋势题（How has X changed...? / What's the future of...?）
过去 → 现在 → 原因/未来
Things have changed dramatically.事情变化巨大
In the past, ... Nowadays, ...过去...现在
This is largely because...这主要是因为
I'm optimistic that...我乐观地认为
This trend shows no sign of slowing down.这个趋势没有放缓迹象
It's still in its early stages.它仍处于早期阶段
We're likely to see ... in the years ahead.未来几年我们可能会看到
D. 建议题（How can we...? / What should...?）
问题 → 建议 → 理由 + 预期效果
The most effective way would be to...最有效的方式是
A good starting point is...一个好的起点是
If this were implemented, ...如果实施的话虚拟语气加分
It would go a long way toward...对...大有帮助
The ball is in the government's court.这得看政府的行动
It's easier said than done, but ...说起来容易做起来难，但
A shift in mindset is needed.需要观念的转变

🔗 万能连接词 & 强调表达

A. 连接词（让回答更连贯，每句一个就够，别堆）
用自然的连接词替代"嗯...然后...还有..."
Having said that, ...话虽如此转折
On top of that, ...除此之外递进
For instance, ... / Take ... for example比如举例
That's largely because ...这主要是因为因果
In a nutshell, ...简而言之总结
Mind you, ...不过话说回来补充修正
Come to think of it, ...仔细一想补充想法
More importantly, ...更重要的是强调
B. 强调程度（让词汇更有冲击力）
替换掉 boring 的 very / really
incredibly / remarkably极其、惊人地
absolutely / utterly完全、彻底
by far the most ...毫无疑问最...
nothing short of ...简直是、不亚于
to a certain extent在某种程度上弱化
on the whole / by and large总的来说
a bit of a ... / somewhat有点、略微弱化
particularly / especially尤其
C. 让步与平衡（体现思辨，Part 3 高分关键）
让考官觉得你"想得全面"，不是非黑即白
It's a bit of a double-edged sword.这有点像双刃剑
There's no one-size-fits-all answer.没有放之四海皆准的答案
It largely depends on the situation.很大程度上取决于情况
I can see both sides of the argument.我能理解双方观点
That being said, I still lean towards ...尽管如此，我仍倾向
It's not as clear-cut as it seems.事情没有表面那么简单

🆘 万能救命句（卡壳时用）

这些句子能帮你争取 2-3 秒思考时间，还显得很自然
That's an interesting question. Let me think for a second.这问题有意思，让我想一下
I've never really thought about that before, but I'd say...我从没想过，但我会说
To be honest, it's a bit hard to say, but...说实话有点难说，但
Well, it depends on the situation, but generally...看情况，但一般
If I had to sum it up in one word, I'd say...用一个词总结就是
What I mean is that...我的意思是（发现自己说错了，用它纠正）
Sorry, let me put it another way.抱歉，换个说法（重新组织语言）
